Growing, Learning and Becoming a Better Version of Yourself

January 14, 2023 by Solay Wellington

Life is a journey of growth and self-discovery. It's about learning from our experiences, embracing challenges, accepting failures, and always striving to become the best version of ourselves. In this blog post, I want to share some insights on how we can grow, learn, and evolve into better individuals. For the past year I have learnt some things about myself and I have grown in ways I never thought possible. I have learned to embrace change, to be more resilient, and to approach life with a positive mindset. Usually when we face challenges, we tend to focus on the negative aspects of the situation. However, I have learned that by focusing on how I can grow and learn from the experience, I can turn setbacks into opportunities for growth. I have also learned the importance of self-reflection and self-awareness. By taking the time to reflect on my thoughts, emotions, and actions, I have gained a deeper understanding of myself and my values. This has allowed me to make more informed decisions and to align my actions with my goals and aspirations.

So I want to start this new chapter of my life by becoming more intentional about everything even the smallest thins we tend to overlook. I want to be more mindful of my thoughts and actions, and to approach life with a sense of purpose and direction. I want to become the best version of myself in academics, career, and personal life. I want to continue to learn and grow, to embrace challenges, and to approach life with a positive mindset. There is so much to this beautiful life and we only get one chance to maximise it and I want to use that chance to the fullest. I want to be a better friend, a better family member, and a better member of my community. I want to make a positive impact on the world around me, and to leave a lasting legacy that reflects my values and aspirations.

Becoming a Better Computer Science Student

January 15, 2023 by Solay Wellington

As a computer science student, I am constantly striving to improve my skills and knowledge. In this blog post, I want to share some insights on how we can become better computer science students. Computer Science is very differet from other degrees, it requires a lot of practice and critical thinking. Knowing what is taught during lectures is not enough, we have to practice and apply what we learn in order to become better students. I have learned that the key to success in computer science is to be proactive and take ownership of our learning. This means seeking out resources, asking questions, and actively engaging with the material. It also means being willing to take risks and make mistakes, as this is often where the most valuable learning occurs. This major is relatively hard and requires a lot of time and effort, but with dedication and perseverance, we can achieve our goals and become successful computer science students. Nothing is impossible, we just have to keep pushing forward and never give up on our dreams. I am excited to continue my journey as a computer science student, and I look forward to the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead.

In conclusion, becoming a better computer science student requires a combination of hard work, dedication, and a willingness to learn. By being proactive, seeking out resources, and actively engaging with the material, we can improve our skills and knowledge and achieve our goals. I am committed to continuing my journey as a computer science student, and I am excited to see where this path will take me in the future.
